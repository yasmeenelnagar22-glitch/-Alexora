أليكسورا | Alexora
Discover Alexandria, One Story at a Time إسكندرية على مزاجك
A Digital Marketing Graduation Project developed as part of the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI).

Project Name
(Alexora | أليكسورا)
Alexora is a tourism-focused digital brand dedicated to helping people discover Alexandria, Egypt.

Project Idea
Alexora is a digital marketing graduation project focused on promoting tourism in Alexandria through engaging storytelling, useful recommendations, and inspiring visual content.

The project helps Egyptian visitors and Alexandria residents discover historical landmarks, coastal attractions, local culture, and hidden gems based on their interests, available time, and budget.

The Problem
People planning a visit or an outing in Alexandria often find information scattered across different sources, making it difficult to choose suitable places and create a clear itinerary.

Our Solution
Develop a tourism brand and an integrated digital marketing campaign that provides accessible content, suggested itineraries, and practical ideas for exploring the city.

Target Audience
- **Primary audience:** Egyptian young adults planning an outing or a short visit to Alexandria.
- **Secondary audience:** Alexandria residents looking for new places and experiences within their city.

Campaign Offer
A free digital guide titled:

**“A Day in Alexandria Your Way | يوم في إسكندرية على مزاجك”**

Main Call to Action
Request or download the free guide.

Project Objectives
- Increase awareness of Alexora and Alexandria’s tourism experiences.
- Create useful and engaging tourism content.
- Build an engaged social media community.
- Encourage users to request or download the free guide.
- Apply digital marketing skills through a practical graduation project.

Planned Deliverables
- Market and competitor research.
- Audience segmentation and buyer personas.
- Brand identity and messaging.
- Digital marketing and social media strategy.
- Content calendar.
- Social media designs and short-form videos.
- A free digital tourism guide.
- SEO keyword research and sample tourism content.
- Paid advertising plans covering objectives, targeting, creatives, and proposed budgets.
- A performance measurement framework.
- A final project report and presentation.

KPIs
The following KPIs will be used to evaluate project performance where implementation and tracking are available.

| Objective | KPI | Measurement Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand Awareness | Reach and impressions | Available platform insights, such as Meta Business Suite. |
| Audience Growth | Net new followers | Followers at the end of the reporting period minus followers at the start. |
| Content Engagement | Engagement rate by reach | Total content interactions ÷ reach × 100, using the same reporting scope. |
| Content Usefulness | Saves and shares | Available post and reel insights. |
| Video Performance | Video views and average watch time, where available | Available video and reel insights. |
| Traffic Generation | Link clicks | Platform link-click metrics or tracked links, where configured. |
| Guide Conversions | Number of guide requests or downloads | Form submissions or tracked download events, where configured. |
| Conversion Efficiency | Guide conversion rate | Completed guide requests or downloads ÷ relevant landing-page visits × 100, where both are tracked. |
| Paid Advertising, if implemented | CTR, CPC, and cost per guide conversion | Advertising platform reports and configured conversion tracking. |
| Project Delivery | Deliverable completion rate | Completed deliverables ÷ planned deliverables × 100. |

Measurement Notes
- Numerical performance targets will be agreed upon before campaign implementation, based on the campaign duration, starting audience, available resources, and any approved advertising budget.
- Actual results will be reported only for activities that have been implemented and measured.
- Paid advertising execution depends on budget availability; preparing a paid campaign plan does not mean that ads have been launched.
- Proposed activities, forecasts, and any simulated examples will be clearly labeled and kept separate from actual results.
- If a metric cannot be tracked, it will be reported as unavailable.
