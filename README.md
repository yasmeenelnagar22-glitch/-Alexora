أليكسورا | Alexora
Alexora — Discover Alexandria, One Story at a Time .Alexora is a digital marketing graduation project focused on promoting tourism in Alexandria, Egypt. It aims to inspire visitors to explore the city’s historical landmarks, coastal attractions, local culture, and hidden gems.The project develops a tourism brand and an integrated digital marketing campaign, combining market research, audience segmentation, brand identity, content creation, social media management, search engine optimization, and paid advertising planning.Our goal is to build brand awareness, encourage meaningful engagement, and help potential visitors discover ideas for their next trip to Alexandria. Project deliverables include: - Market and competitor researc - Target audience profiles and buyer personas- Brand identity and messaging- Social media strategy and content calendar- Social media posts and short-form videos- SEO keyword research and sample tourism content- Paid campaign plans, including objectives, targeting, and budgets- A performance measurement framework and final presentation Campaign results will be reported where campaigns are implemented. Proposed activities and simulated results will be clearly identified.
